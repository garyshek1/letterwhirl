Privacy Policy

Letter Whirl
Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how the Letter Whirl iOS app handles information. Letter Whirl is a letter-catching game. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not send your score or sound setting to us.

Who we are

Letter Whirl is published for iOS. For privacy questions, email letterwhirl@homayho.com.

Information the app handles

Play stays on your device. Your score, the round in progress, and whether sound is muted exist only while the app is open. Closing the app clears that session. The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.

The app does not use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not read the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA).

Advertising

Letter Whirl shows a banner ad through Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google and companies that help deliver or measure that ad may receive information from your device, such as:

  • IP address, which can indicate a coarse location such as a city or region
  • Device type, operating system, language, and general app information
  • Ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped
  • A consent or opt-out choice stored on the device

Google uses this information to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance, and detect invalid traffic. How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google’s privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy, and its advertising information is at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Apple’s SKAdNetwork may send Apple an aggregated report about whether an ad led to an install. That report is not a profile of you that we receive.

We do not sell personal information for money. Showing ads can involve sharing device information with advertising partners. Some US state laws treat that sharing as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information.

Your ad choices

On each launch, the app asks Google’s User Messaging Platform whether a privacy choice is required before an ad can load.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app can show a consent form before the round starts. You can refuse. You can change or withdraw that choice later with the Privacy button in the game, when that button is shown.
  • In US states that require an opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information, the Privacy button opens that choice. It includes “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information.”
  • Elsewhere, no privacy form is required and the game starts after a short check.

The consent tool stores your choice on the device so it can be reused on later launches. Deleting the app removes that local copy.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, personalized ads and storing or reading ad-related information on your device rely on your consent. You can withdraw consent with the Privacy button. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before the withdrawal.

Where a US state privacy law applies, you can opt out of sale or sharing for cross-context advertising with the Privacy button.

Children

Letter Whirl is a general-audience game. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, email letterwhirl@homayho.com and we will follow up.

Retention and transfers

We do not run a server that stores player profiles or gameplay. Advertising partners keep information under their own policies. Google may process advertising information in the United States and other countries where it operates.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, to opt out of sale or sharing, and to appeal a decision on a privacy request. Email letterwhirl@homayho.com and we will respond. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because we do not keep an account or a gameplay record, we may not be able to identify information about you beyond what is stored on your device and what our advertising partners hold. For information Google holds, use Google’s own privacy tools linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after the new date means the updated policy applies.
